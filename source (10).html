State of Hillview Regulations

In State of Hillview, rules are in place to ensure an immersive and fair role-playing experience. Read below for a summary of key regulations.

  • New Life Rule (NLR): Avoid re-engaging in scenarios that led to your demise.
  • Random DeathMatch (RDM): No unjustified killing.
  • Leaving/Murder/Resetting to Avoid Arrest (LTAA/MTAA/RTAA) are prohibited.
  • Spawn Killing, Ambushing RP, and Fail Role Play (FRP) are not allowed.
  • Infractions are subject to warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans.

  • Trolling, cop baiting, and toxicity are not permitted.
  • Exploiting glitches, threats, and stealing assets are strictly forbidden.
  • Alternate accounts to bypass rules are prohibited.
  • Players must adhere to Roblox Terms of Service.

  • No toxicity, harassment, or hate speech.
  • Respectful communication and no spamming.
  • Follow staff instructions and post in appropriate channels.
  • Any violation may result in immediate bans.
